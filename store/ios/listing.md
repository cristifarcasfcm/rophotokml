## Notes for Review (englezește, câmp obligatoriu)
```
ROPhotoKml photographs agricultural parcels in the field. No account or login is required.
To test:
1. Download sample-parcels.kml from https://github.com/cristifarcasfcm/rophotokml/blob/main/store/sample-parcels.kml (or open it from Files with "Open in ROPhotoKml").
2. In the app tap KML (top-left) → Add KML file → choose it. Two fictional parcels appear near Baia Mare, Romania.
3. Tap the GPS button and the Camera button (allow permissions). Tap a parcel → popup.
4. The shutter unlocks only within 10 m of a parcel; to test anywhere, tap the "Buffer" button to turn the 10 m check off, then take a photo and tap Save: the photo (with GPS EXIF and a text overlay) is saved to the Photos library.
Native functionality: camera capture, GPS, compass (motion sensors), saving to Photos via a native plugin, registered .kml document type ("Open in"), persistent local storage, offline operation of all features except map tiles.
Third-party network use: map tiles (Esri, OpenStreetMap) and one reverse-geocoding request per session (OpenStreetMap Nominatim). No data is collected on our servers; there are none.
```
